SECTION 01

Purpose and Scope of Platform Services

Recycle Hub operates an enterprise technology infrastructure for sorting, cataloging, collecting, and channeling post-industrial and post-consumer recyclable raw materials to verified processing plants.

  • Inventory Listing: Sourcing Partners leverage Recycle Hub's platform to list verified recyclable inventory.
  • Logistics Dispatch: Schedule verified pickup windows with dedicated enterprise logistics fleets.
  • Digital Settlement: Receive direct digital payouts for compliant recyclable commodities upon weighment verification.
SECTION 02

Partner Qualification & Eligibility

To qualify as an active Sourcing Partner, the entity or individual warrants that:

  • It possesses full legal capacity and regulatory permits to conduct commercial trade in recyclable commodities.
  • All submitted business credentials, tax identification numbers, and contact details are authentic and up to date.
  • It maintains continuous compliance with local environmental and municipal waste-handling regulations.
SECTION 03

Account Credentials & Access Control

Sourcing Partners are strictly responsible for maintaining access security over their account credentials:

  • Credential Security: Multi-factor authentication and access credentials must be kept confidential at all times.
  • Authorized Use: Any dispatch order or commercial transaction submitted under authenticated partner credentials shall be deemed authorized by the Partner.
SECTION 04

Title, Legal Ownership & Warranties

Ownership and title transfers are governed under the following explicit legal terms:

  • Warranty of Ownership: The Partner explicitly represents that all recyclable inventory presented is free of encumbrances, liens, or third-party disputes.
  • Title Transfer Node: Title to materials transfers to Recycle Hub upon physical inspection and digital weighment slip issuance.
SECTION 05

Accepted Material Specifications

Recycle Hub accepts verified commodities adhering to regional quality grades:

  • Paper & Fiber: OCC corrugated cardboard, Kraft paper, and sorted graphic paper grades.
  • Polymers: Industrial HDPE, PET bottles/flakes, LDPE film, and rigid PP materials.
  • Metals: Ferrous scrap, aluminum alloys, copper, and segregated industrial metal scrap.
  • E-Waste: Segregated electronic components and circuit board assemblies.
SECTION 06

Prohibited & Hazardous Substances

Recycle Hub strictly prohibits consignment of restricted hazardous materials:

  • Prohibited: Hazardous chemical residues, toxic solvents, or contaminated containers.
  • Prohibited: Bio-medical waste, pharmaceutical residues, or infectious materials.
  • Prohibited: Explosive compounds, pressurized gas cylinders, or radioactive components.
Regulatory Compliance Note: Presentation of prohibited hazardous waste triggers immediate account freezing and statutory notification to environmental enforcement bodies.
SECTION 07

Logistics, Dispatch & Scheduling

Collection operations follow optimized logistics protocols:

  • Dispatch Scheduling: Timetables are assigned based on regional fleet density and pickup volume.
  • Site Access: The Partner agrees to provide safe, unimpeded access for heavy collection vehicles at the loading bay.
SECTION 08

Quality Verification & Moisture Adjustments

Quality inspections are conducted at physical loading nodes:

  • Grade Assessment: Field inspectors evaluate purity, contamination level, and moisture percentage.
  • Manifest Deductions: Deductions for excess moisture or non-recyclable contamination are transparently itemized on the digital manifest slip.
SECTION 09

Commercial Settlement & Payout Terms

Financial settlements are executed automatically upon manifest confirmation:

  • Dynamic Pricing: Settlement rates map directly to index-benchmarked daily market commodity tariffs.
  • Fast Remittance: Payouts are transferred via NEFT/RTGS or UPI within 24 hours of manifest approval.
Settlement Guarantee: All verified pickup manifests are backed by Recycle Hub's Automated Fast-Track Payment Guarantee.
SECTION 10

Partner Standards of Conduct

  • Partners warrant fair dealing and professional communication with logistics operators and inspectors.
  • Tampering with digital weighment scales or falsifying manifest weights is strictly prohibited.
SECTION 11

Cancellation Policies

  • Pickups may be canceled up to 2 hours prior to fleet dispatch without penalty.
  • Frequent unannounced cancellations may incur nominal dispatch recovery fees.
SECTION 12

Data Protection & Privacy

  • Partner operational logs and contact records are encrypted with AES-256 protocols.
  • Transaction history is processed in accordance with national enterprise data security standards.
SECTION 13

Intellectual Property Rights

All software platforms, pricing algorithms, brand marks, and dispatch systems remain the exclusive intellectual property of Recycle Hub Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

SECTION 14

Term & Account Suspension

  • Either party may terminate platform participation with 7 days written electronic notice.
  • Immediate account suspension applies in instances of regulatory non-compliance, fraud, or safety breaches.
SECTION 15

Limitations of Liability

In no event shall Recycle Hub be liable for indirect or consequential damages exceeding total platform service fees accumulated over the prior 30-day billing cycle.

SECTION 16

Force Majeure

Operational delays caused by natural disasters, severe weather events, transit blockades, or emergency statutory directives shall suspend performance obligations without penalty.

SECTION 17

Contractual Amendments

Contract revisions will be published on the legal vault portal with 14 days advance notification sent to registered partner accounts.

SECTION 18

Governing Law & Dispute Resolution

  • This Agreement is governed by the laws of India.
  • Any unresolved commercial dispute shall be referred to sole arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act.
SECTION 19

Official Notices

Formal notices must be delivered electronically to legal@recyclehub.com or to the registered partner email on file.
