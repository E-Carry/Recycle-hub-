FAQ

Questions, answered.

Who can join Recycle Hub?
Businesses that collect recyclable material (sourcing partners) and businesses that process it (industrial partners). Every account is verified before it can trade.
How are rates decided?
The Recycle Hub desk publishes benchmark rates per grade, with trend and demand status. Rates you see on this page come from the same live feed your dashboard uses.
Which materials are accepted?
Metals (copper, steel, aluminium, brass), plastics (PET, HDPE), paper, glass and e-waste. Each grade lists its purity standard and minimum lot size.
How do pickups work?
Sourcing partners pick a date and time slot from their dashboard. An EV pickup is assigned and you can track it live until weighing.
Can I talk to someone?
Rio answers most questions instantly. For anything else, email recycle.hub.pvt.ltd@gmail.com.
