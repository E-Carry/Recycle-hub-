How it works

From registration to payout in four steps.

The same flow for both sides of the trade: verify once, then every pickup or order runs through your dashboard with a paper trail attached.

  1. 01

    Register and verify

    Choose sourcing or industrial, then verify your business with OTP and KYC.

  2. 02

    List or source

    Book a pickup slot or place an order against the published, graded rates.

  3. 03

    Track the EV

    Follow the pickup vehicle live until the load reaches the weighbridge.

  4. 04

    Weigh, invoice, settle

    Net weight is confirmed, the GST invoice is generated and payment is settled.

Two partner types

Built for both sides of the trade.

Sourcing partners bring material in. Industrial partners take graded material out. Each gets a dashboard built around their side of the deal.

Sourcing partner

You collect material. We buy it.

For aggregators, scrap dealers and collection fleets selling recyclable material.

  • Published rates per grade, with trend and demand, before you book.
  • EV pickups on your schedule: pick a date and slot, then track the vehicle.
  • Paid on weighbridge weight, with the invoice in your dashboard.
  • Rio AI answers rate, pickup and payout questions any time.

Industrial partner

You process material. We supply it.

For mills, smelters, recyclers and manufacturers buying graded recycled input.

  • Graded lots with purity specs and minimum lot sizes stated up front.
  • Orders and inventory in one dashboard, with delivery status.
  • HSN codes and GST on every order, so procurement paperwork is done.
  • Verified suppliers: material comes only from KYC-checked partners.
